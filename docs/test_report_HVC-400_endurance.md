# Test Report TR-2026-121: Endurance and Overload Test HVC-400 (fictional sample document)

Three samples (S1, S2, S3) of the HVC-400 contactor were tested.

In the mechanical endurance test, samples S1 and S2 completed 200,000 switching cycles without failure.

Contact resistance of S1 increased from 0.08 mΩ before the test to 0.11 mΩ after the test. Contact resistance of S2 increased from 0.09 mΩ to 0.13 mΩ. Both values are below the limit of 0.2 mΩ.

Sample S3 was used for the overload test at 600 A. Contact welding occurred on S3 after 1,850 cycles, so sample S3 failed the overload test.

The welded contacts of S3 were examined with a digital microscope. The images show material transfer on the movable contact.

Recommended action: repeat the overload test with two new samples from the next production batch.

The test was carried out from 2 to 9 October 2026.
