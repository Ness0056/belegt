# Datasheet: HVC-400 High-Voltage Contactor (fictional sample document)

The HVC-400 is a sealed high-voltage DC contactor for battery disconnect units in electric vehicles.

Rated operating voltage is 800 V DC. The maximum continuous current is 400 A at an ambient temperature of 85 °C.

The coil is designed for a nominal voltage of 12 V DC. Pick-up voltage is at most 8 V and drop-out voltage is at least 1 V.

Initial contact resistance is specified as at most 0.2 mΩ at 400 A.

The mechanical life is specified as 200,000 switching cycles without load. The electrical life at 400 A and 800 V is specified as 5,000 cycles.

The insulation resistance between open contacts must be at least 100 MΩ at 1,000 V DC.

The housing is filled with a gas mixture to suppress arcing. The operating temperature range is −40 °C to 85 °C.
